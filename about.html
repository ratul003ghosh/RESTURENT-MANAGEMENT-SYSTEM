Our Story & Philosophy

A kitchen and a dining floor that operate as one connected, effortless system.

Our Culinary Vision

Built around a simple idea: Service should feel effortless.

RMS Restaurant was built around a simple idea: service should feel effortless on both sides of the pass. Admins manage staff and tables, waiters run the floor, chefs fire tickets on time, and guests can browse, book, and order ahead - all from one connected system.

Founded in 2015, we combine artisanal gourmet dining with real-time order tracking and online time-slot table reservations.

Real-time order tracking from kitchen to table

Fresh Ingredients Daily

We source local organic produce, premium cuts, and fresh seafood every morning to ensure every dish is prepared to perfection.

Effortless Reservations

Pick your table location, date, and exact time slot with live status updates before you step through our doors.

Connected Kitchen & Staff
