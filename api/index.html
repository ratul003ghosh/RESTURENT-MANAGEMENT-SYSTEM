Our Story

A kitchen and a floor that work as one system.

RMS Restaurant was built around a simple idea: service should feel effortless on both sides of the pass. Admins manage staff and tables, waiters run the floor, chefs fire tickets on time, and guests can browse, book, and order ahead - all from one connected system.
